About Me

My name is Christopher J Pishaki, and I am a software engineer building scalable, production-grade software — professionally since 2021, and across an active portfolio of open-source projects spanning developer tools, native macOS utilities, games, and healthcare research.

Tackling unique and novel problems with eagerness and positivity is my modus operandi. I thrive in environments where curiosity is encouraged and innovative thinking is the norm. From implementing comprehensive CI/CD pipelines to building cross-platform mobile applications, I approach each challenge with enthusiasm and technical precision.

As a person, I enjoy connecting with others and learning about their experiences and perspectives. This curiosity has helped me develop a broad worldview and an empathetic approach to problem-solving—qualities that translate directly into better software design and team collaboration.

A formative milestone was building the Integrated Report Processor at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory for Europa Clipper testbed engineers. These days my energy goes into shipping and maintaining seven flagship open-source projects — from a notarized macOS utility to a clinical-safety research platform — alongside professional backend and mobile work.

I am excited to keep building in the open — developer tooling, native macOS apps, and software that takes safety seriously.
