03 / Methodology

What the numbers mean

Diagnostic credit · 0–1

The arithmetic mean of saved automatic diagnosis credits among scored answers, including partial credit. This is not binary accuracy. A skipped or missing answer is unscored, never a zero. Each row reports its own denominator; groups are not pooled into an overall score.

Critical evidence retained

Critical log items retained / critical items in the original input. This measures evidence availability after reduction, not whether a response cites evidence correctly. It is a separate dimension; even an unscored model call can have a measured retained-input count.

Strategies and budgets

raw uses the unreduced input; error_window selects context around errors. 8k is the declared token budget and unrestricted has no budget cap. Token estimates use the saved estimator, not a claim of exact tokenizer counts. Retaining evidence alone does not guarantee diagnosis.

Versions are boundaries

The pilots preserve scoring-v3, diag-v1 and response-v1 results without rescoring; the November 16 observations use scoring-v4, diag-v2 and response-v2. New engine or scoring versions do not rewrite old observations. A changed source revision, fixture pool, size, seed, version or execution option breaks comparability.

Exact requirements for a connecting line

The page computes comparability from source revision; scenario pool, sizes, fixture hashes and seed; dataset, prompt, response and scoring versions; token estimator; generation, strategy-option and provider-extra-argument fingerprints; provider; effort; repetitions; strategy; budget; benchmark and metric. Every field must be known and match. A curator-provided cohort label cannot override this rule. Unknown values never compare as known. Only scored observations on distinct dates can be connected.

Coverage is deliberately small: no confidence intervals, general performance rankings or release-over-time conclusions are supported. More observations under the same declared evaluation conditions are needed to assess a trend.
